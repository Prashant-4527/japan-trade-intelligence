## Key Finding 1 - The 2022 Energy-Currency Crisis

Japan recorded its largest trade deficit of the decade in 2022, 
reaching -$152bn. This crisis resulted from two simultaneous shocks:
the yen falling to 32-year low, making all imports more expensive,
and Russia's invasion of Ukraine driving gloabl energy price sharply upward. Because Japan imports approximately 90% of its energy need, both shock compunded - Japan paid more yen per barrel AND more dollars per barrel for the same energy at the same time. By 2023 the deficit narrowed to -$34bn as energy prices stabilized, but Japan's structural exposure to this exact scenario remains unchanged.
